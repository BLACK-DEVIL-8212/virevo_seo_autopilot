Our Story

Virevo was founded in 2021 by Vikram Sharma and Priya Patel, two IIT Bangalore alumni who spent years working with large retail chains in India and abroad. They saw firsthand how Indian retailers struggled with outdated systems, manual processes, and lack of visibility into their businesses.

Determined to change this, they built Virevo - a modern, cloud-based POS platform that puts the power of enterprise-grade retail technology into the hands of every Indian retailer. From a small team in Bangalore, Virevo has grown to serve over 50,000 retailers across 150+ cities.

Today, Virevo processes over ₹5,000 Crores in monthly transactions, helping retailers save time, reduce costs, and grow their sales. We're committed to continuing our mission of digitizing Indian retail one store at a time.
